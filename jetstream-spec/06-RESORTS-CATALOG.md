## How Resorts Drive Gameplay

1. **Destination page**: shows resorts filtered by proximity to current location or upcoming events
2. **Booking flow**: pick suite, dates, experiences → see total cost → confirm
3. **Booking integration with flights**: "Fly to Tokyo + check into Aman Tokyo" is a combined flow
4. **Active booking status** shows on profile: "Checked into Aman Tokyo, Suite type: Aman Suite, 3 nights remaining"
5. **Prestige bonus** for staying at Tier 4+ properties (+5 per stay)
6. **Friends know**: if you're at Hotel du Cap and a friend is nearby, they DM to meet up
7. **Amenity calls**: "Order the private tea ceremony" is a one-tap experience purchase from the booking detail screen
